# The Evidence

The following sections present the analytical evidence generated for this study.

Each analytical run evaluates the same household, retirement objectives, and planning assumptions while varying the assumptions used to model future investment returns. Together, these analytical runs provide multiple, complementary lines of evidence describing how the proposed retirement strategy responds to a range of plausible market environments.

The evidence is organized to support direct comparison across analytical methods, market environments, and statistical summaries. Presenting a common set of retirement planning metrics for every analytical run allows similarities, differences, and sensitivities to be evaluated consistently.

The purpose of the evidence is not to predict future market performance. Rather, it is to provide an objective body of observations from which the robustness, sensitivity, and tradeoffs of the proposed retirement strategy can be assessed.

The sections that follow present the analytical results without interpretation or recommendation. Guidance for interpreting the evidence and developing planning recommendations is provided in the subsequent sections.
