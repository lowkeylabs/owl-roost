# OWL Overview

OWL is the retirement planning engine used by ROOST to evaluate retirement strategies.

Given a household, planning assumptions, and a set of retirement planning objectives, OWL performs the underlying financial analysis required to estimate retirement outcomes. These outcomes include projected spending, taxes, account balances, withdrawals, and other planning metrics over the lifetime of the retirement plan.

ROOST uses OWL to generate the analytical evidence contained within this package.

## Optimization

OWL solves constrained retirement planning problems.

Depending upon the planning objective, OWL may determine a spending level, withdrawal strategy, Roth conversion strategy, retirement date, or other planning decision that best satisfies the specified optimization goals while respecting the financial and tax constraints of the household.

Each OWL analysis represents one complete evaluation of a retirement strategy under a particular set of assumptions.

## Inputs

OWL requires information describing the household and the planning problem, including items such as:

* household demographics;
* financial assets and liabilities;
* income sources;
* expected spending;
* tax assumptions;
* retirement goals; and
* optimization objectives.

These inputs remain fixed within an analytical run unless explicitly varied as part of the study design.

## Outputs

For each analytical run, OWL produces a comprehensive set of retirement planning metrics.

These metrics may include projected annual spending, lifetime spending, taxes, withdrawals, account balances, Roth conversions, estate values, and other measures relevant to the planning objective.

ROOST summarizes selected OWL outputs within this evidence package to support comparison across multiple analytical runs.

## OWL and ROOST

OWL and ROOST perform complementary roles.

OWL performs the retirement planning analysis.

ROOST organizes, compares, and presents the resulting evidence.

ROOST may execute many OWL analyses using different assumptions or methodologies to evaluate the robustness of a proposed retirement strategy. The resulting body of evidence enables planners and reviewers to understand how retirement outcomes change as assumptions change.

## Role Within This Evidence Package

The numerical results presented throughout this package are generated by OWL.

The organization of the studies, comparison of analytical runs, interpretation of the evidence, and planning questions are provided by ROOST.

Together, OWL and ROOST support evidence-based retirement planning by separating analytical computation from the interpretation and evaluation of retirement planning decisions.
