# ROOST Overview

ROOST is an evidence generation system for retirement planning.

Its purpose is to help planners, households, and large language models evaluate retirement planning decisions by generating structured evidence from multiple analytical perspectives.

Rather than attempting to predict the future or recommend a single optimal course of action, ROOST assembles evidence describing how a proposed retirement strategy performs under a variety of assumptions and conditions.

## Evidence Rather Than Recommendations

Retirement planning decisions involve uncertainty.

Future investment returns, inflation, tax policy, longevity, healthcare costs, and household spending needs cannot be known with certainty. As a result, retirement planning rarely has a single objectively correct answer.

ROOST does not attempt to eliminate this uncertainty.

Instead, it generates evidence that helps planners understand the robustness, sensitivity, and tradeoffs associated with alternative retirement planning decisions.

The responsibility for interpreting the evidence and making recommendations remains with the reviewer.

## Evidence-Based Planning

ROOST evaluates retirement strategies by generating multiple analytical runs under different assumptions and methodologies.

Collectively, these runs form a body of evidence that allows the reviewer to distinguish between outcomes that are consistently supported and those that depend upon particular assumptions or favorable conditions.

The objective is not to identify the analytical run producing the most desirable outcome.

Instead, the objective is to determine whether a proposed retirement strategy remains appropriate across a broad range of plausible future environments.

## The Role of the Reviewer

This evidence package is intended to support informed decision making.

The reviewer should evaluate the evidence presented, identify important patterns, assess the robustness of the proposed retirement strategy, and explain the principal risks, tradeoffs, and uncertainties.

Recommendations should be supported by the evidence contained within this package rather than by unsupported assumptions or predictions regarding future market performance.

## Annual Planning

Retirement planning is an ongoing process rather than a one-time optimization exercise.

This evidence package represents one planning cycle within that continuing process. As household circumstances, investment performance, tax laws, and retirement objectives evolve, future planning cycles may produce different evidence and different recommendations.

Accordingly, recommendations should be appropriate for the current planning cycle while recognizing that retirement planning decisions are periodically revisited as new information becomes available.
