# How Much Can I Spend in an Uncertain Market?

## Purpose

One of the first questions retirees ask is:

> **How much can I reasonably spend?**

The answer depends upon future investment returns, which are inherently uncertain and cannot be predicted with certainty.

A spending level that appears sustainable under one sequence of market returns may prove unsustainable under another. Likewise, an overly conservative spending strategy may unnecessarily reduce quality of life if future markets perform better than expected.

Because no single market forecast can be relied upon, retirement planning should not attempt to identify the one mathematically "optimal" spending level. Instead, planning should identify spending decisions that remain appropriate across a wide range of plausible future market environments.

This evidence package evaluates the proposed retirement strategy under multiple historical market regimes and stochastic market simulations. Rather than predicting the future, the evidence illustrates how retirement outcomes change as market conditions change.

The objective is to evaluate the **robustness** of the proposed spending strategy, identify the uncertainty surrounding future outcomes, and determine whether the recommendation remains appropriate across many plausible futures.

## Interpreting the Evidence

When reviewing the evidence, focus on planning decisions rather than individual numerical results.

In particular:

* Examine how recommended spending changes across multiple market environments.
* Compare not only median outcomes, but also lower and upper uncertainty bounds (for example, p10, median, and p90).
* Look for broad ranges of spending that produce similar retirement outcomes rather than searching for a single numerical optimum.
* Distinguish between statistically observable differences and differences that are large enough to influence a planning decision.
* Consider both the expected outcome and the confidence that the evidence provides for the recommendation.

A recommendation supported by many market environments and a broad range of uncertainty is generally more reliable than one that performs best under only a narrow set of assumptions.

## Questions

The evidence presented in this package is intended to help answer questions such as:

* How much can this household reasonably spend given uncertainty in future investment returns?
* How sensitive is the proposed spending level to unfavorable market conditions?
* Which market environments place the greatest stress on the retirement plan?
* Does the recommended spending level remain appropriate across multiple historical and simulated market environments?
* How much uncertainty exists in future spending, taxes, and terminal wealth?
* Is there a broad range of spending decisions that produce materially similar outcomes, or does the recommendation depend upon a narrow optimum?
* How much confidence does the evidence provide for the recommended spending strategy?
