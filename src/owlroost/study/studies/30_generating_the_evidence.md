# Generating the Evidence

The evidence presented in this package was intentionally generated using multiple, independent lines of evidence rather than relying on a single assumption about future investment returns.

Future investment returns cannot be predicted with certainty. Consequently, no single historical period, mathematical model, or analytical methodology can fully characterize the uncertainty facing a retirement plan.

Instead, this study evaluates the proposed retirement strategy using several complementary approaches to understanding future market behavior. Each analytical approach provides a different perspective on how the retirement plan responds to uncertainty.

## Multiple Lines of Evidence

Each analytical method contributes a distinct line of evidence.

Some methods evaluate retirement outcomes using specific historical market environments.

Others evaluate many plausible sequences of future investment returns using stochastic techniques.

Still others provide deterministic reference cases that illustrate how retirement outcomes respond to different long-term return assumptions.

Each approach has strengths and limitations. No individual method should be interpreted as providing the definitive answer.

Instead, the evidence should be considered collectively. Confidence in a planning recommendation increases when multiple independent analytical methods support similar conclusions despite differing assumptions.

## Why Multiple Methods Matter

A retirement strategy that performs well under only one set of market assumptions may not remain appropriate under different future conditions.

Conversely, a strategy that produces consistently acceptable outcomes across multiple analytical methods is generally more robust because its success does not depend upon any particular view of future market performance.

Agreement among independent analytical methods provides stronger evidence than agreement within a single method alone. When different approaches reach similar conclusions, confidence in the resulting planning recommendation increases.

The objective of this study is therefore not to determine which analytical method is "correct" or which produces the most favorable outcome.

Rather, the objective is to evaluate whether the proposed retirement strategy remains appropriate across a broad range of plausible future market environments.

## Evidence in This Package

Each analytical run evaluates the same household, planning objectives, and baseline assumptions. Differences among the runs primarily reflect differences in how future investment returns are modeled.

The analytical runs that follow should be viewed as complementary lines of evidence rather than competing predictions of the future.

Taken together, they provide the foundation for evaluating the robustness, sensitivity, and consistency of the proposed retirement strategy. Subsequent sections present the analytical methods, summarize the resulting outcomes, and provide guidance for interpreting the evidence as a whole.
